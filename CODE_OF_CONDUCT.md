# Code of conduct

nodeyard is built by and for people who run computers at home for fun,
learning and usefulness. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull
requests, discussions or anywhere else in the project) to feel welcome,
whatever their experience, background or identity.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to Linux, networking or
  Kubernetes; questions are welcome and "obvious" is relative.
- Be respectful when you disagree. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give credit, accept feedback gracefully, and own your mistakes.
- Keep discussions on topic and useful to the people who will read them
  later.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behaviour, including about age, body,
  disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, experience level,
  nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity or
  orientation.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberately disrupting discussions, or encouraging others to do any of
  the above.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately via
the email address on <https://github.com/Codemanhtmlpythoncss>, or through
a private security advisory if that's easier
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)). Reports are kept confidential.

## What happens next

The maintainers will review the report and respond in a way they judge
fair: from a private word, to a public warning, to a temporary or
permanent ban from the project's spaces. We'll tell the reporter what was
done where we can.

These guidelines are inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
